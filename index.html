01 About Me

I'm a student at GUTECH in Karachi, studying computer science. I picked up mechatronics and Arduino at The Lab School, and I like building things that actually work: robots that follow lines and push opponents out of a ring, a water level controller, and parts I design myself in Tinkercad and print on a 3D printer.

I work with Chaajao, an educational platform. I helped build version 2.0, researched new features, and helped design the app and web version in Figma. I also shoot, edit and publish videos and posts for Chaajao SAT's social media channels.

Robotics competitions have been a big part of my journey. I represented Pakistan at the FIRST Global Challenge in 2020, volunteered at Robotron 1.0 and 2.0, and came back to Robotron '26 as an organizer and event judge.

★ Competitions & Recognition

FIRST GLOBAL CHALLENGE // 14 AUG 2020

Rube Goldberg Machine for FIRST Global

My school and I were selected to represent Pakistan at the FIRST Global Challenge in the Technical Challenge on 14 August 2020. The task: transport a ball from one point to another using a Rube Goldberg machine, a chain-reaction contraption that completes a simple job in an elaborate way. We placed 15th on the Global Leaderboard.

  • Selected to represent Pakistan on the global stage
  • Ranked 15th on the Global Leaderboard
  • Chain reaction built from a 3D printed car, marbles, an air blower and a water turbine
